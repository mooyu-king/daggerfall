Daggerfall Online privacy policy

How this community site handles locally selected game files, browser storage, traffic analytics, support requests, and community account services.

Scope and current status

This policy covers daggerfall.online and its self-hosted client. It covers our independent account and multiplayer services. It does not describe or govern the separate daggerfalljs.dev service.

Original game files and local storage

The game client runs at s.daggerfall.online inside this website. It reads files that you select on your device and may cache game data, preferences, and local progress in browser storage belonging to that subdomain. Original ARENA2 files are not uploaded to our server by the intended import flow.

Clearing site data can remove local progress. Storage availability and retention depend on your browser and operating system. Do not clear data before preserving a valid save.

Traffic analytics

The website and embedded game load https://data.1back.link/js/app.js and send analytics requests to https://data.1back.link/api/send to help understand page use. Requests may include ordinary network and browser information, page addresses, and referral information.

Browser settings and blockers may prevent these requests. Do not put credentials or personal information in page URLs. We do not intentionally include game files, passwords, recovery codes, or save contents in analytics events.

Advertising

Production website pages load a third-party popunder advertising script from abscloud.org. An interaction may open an advertisement in a separate tab or window. The provider may process network and browser information, referral data, ad interactions, cookies or similar identifiers to deliver and measure advertising. Advertisement content is controlled by the provider and is not an endorsement by this website.

Advertising is disabled in our local development mode. The website does not intentionally send original game files, passwords, recovery codes or save contents to the advertising script.

Accounts and online play

Online features process account identifiers, authentication records, selected cloud saves, social information and operational data needed to provide the service. Contact us about access or deletion requests; do not send passwords or recovery codes in email.

We do not claim that online play is anonymous or that network connections reveal no technical information. No payment or subscription service is offered in this version.

Support and external links

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Send the minimum useful information. External services such as Steam and GitHub have their own policies.

Questions and changes

Contact mooyuking@gmail.com about privacy, a support message, or a deletion request. We will verify relevant ownership before acting on account-related requests for this community service. Material changes will be reflected in the policy date.
