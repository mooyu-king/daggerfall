AT THE TRAILHEAD

Questions before you leave

Do I need my Daggerfall files?

No. This separate Aetheria adventure loads its own resources. No ARENA2 files or Daggerfall character are used.

Which class suits a first expedition?

Warden offers the most starting health. Ranger favors movement and distance. Star Mage trades health for higher base damage. Choose your class at the start of the game.

Is the original online world connected?

No. This is an independent solo adaptation. Original Aetheria accounts, parties, guilds and PvP are separate from the game on this page.

Can I resume on another device?

Export a JSON save from the pause menu, then import it on the other device. There is no cloud account, and embedded saves may be separated by website.
