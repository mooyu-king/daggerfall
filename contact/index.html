Reach the project

Email mooyuking@gmail.com. This address is for the independent daggerfall.online project; we cannot administer accounts belonging to Bethesda, Steam, or the upstream DFO Online service.

For a game or website problem

Include your device, browser version, page address, what you expected, and what happened. A few reproducible steps are more useful than a large file attachment. Check Service status and Troubleshooting first.

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, session tokens, original game archives, or complete saves unless a specific, appropriate support process has been agreed.

Corrections and rights inquiries

Identify the page or material, explain the concern, and provide a way to follow up. We welcome corrections to version information and accessibility feedback. Response times are not guaranteed.
