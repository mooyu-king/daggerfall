A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you enter.

What is DFO Online (dfoonline)?+

DFO Online is the open-source browser multiplayer project behind this client. If you searched for dfoonline, this is an independent Daggerfall Online integration, with separate service configuration and no shared upstream accounts. Follow our multiplayer guide to create an online character and meet friends.

Is this the official Daggerfall website?+

No. This is an independent community project based on the open-source DFO Online client. We are not Bethesda, ZeniMax, or the upstream development team.

Do I need the original game?+

You need your own Daggerfall data files. The original is available free on Steam. Our client reads the files you select locally; we do not provide ARENA2.

Can I play with friends right now?+

Yes. Create separate online characters, use World chat to find each other, then Invite to party and Accept. Leave the solo Privateer’s Hold introduction separately and meet outside. Our multiplayer guide shows the steps.

Can I use a phone or tablet?+

The client includes touch controls. Start in landscape and use Open full page if needed. For the easiest first setup, use desktop Chrome or Edge with a keyboard and mouse.
